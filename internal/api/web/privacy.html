Privacy at Kaqen

Kaqen is operated by Ploos AS. You do not need an account to generate logo assets.

Your uploads

Uploaded PNG, WebP and restricted SVG files are processed to inspect your logo or generate a downloadable ZIP. Kaqen does not intentionally persist uploaded logos or generated assets in an application database.

Uploads may be buffered in temporary files by the HTTP multipart parser during processing. The application removes these temporary files when the request finishes. Server infrastructure and reverse-proxy logging must be configured separately.

Browser and server data

The generator does not require third-party analytics or advertising scripts. Normal web-server access logs may contain IP addresses, request paths and timestamps depending on deployment configuration. Retention and access controls must be documented before public launch.

Questions

For privacy inquiries, contact the operator, Ploos AS, through its published company contact channels.
